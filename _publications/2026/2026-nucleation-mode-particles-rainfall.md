abstract: >-
  This study reports a nucleation-mode particle burst during a convective rainfall event at a coastal site in Qingdao, China. The results suggest that wet scavenging, downward transport, ozone intrusion, and enhanced atmospheric oxidation capacity jointly contributed to the observed near-surface particle burst.
