In 2021, I participated in a chamber experiment investigating emissions from **green tide algae** under controlled conditions.

Green tide algae are important biological materials in coastal environments. Their photochemical processes may influence reactive nitrogen and halogon chemistry.

This experiment was an important step in my research on marine-related HONO sources and helped connect coastal algal processes with atmospheric oxidation chemistry.
