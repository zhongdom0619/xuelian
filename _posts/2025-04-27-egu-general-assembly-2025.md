I presented a poster entitled **"Atmospheric Nitrous Acid in Contrasting Environments in China"** in the session "AS3.36 Advances in the measurement and modelling of radicals and their precursors in the troposphere" at the EGU General Assembly 2025 in Vienna, Austria.

The poster presented my research on atmospheric HONO in contrasting environments in China, with a focus on HONO sources, budgets, and atmospheric implications under different environmental conditions.
