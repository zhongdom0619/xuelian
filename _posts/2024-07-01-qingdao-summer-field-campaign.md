In summer of 2024, I led a coastal field measurement campaign in **Qingdao**, focusing on atmospheric pollutants under the influence of coastal algae.

The main objective of this campaign was to investigate how algae-influenced coastal environments may affect atmospheric composition, especially reactive nitrogen species, HONO, and related pollutants.

This was my first experience leading a field campaign. I was involved in organizing field operations, coordinating measurements, maintaining instruments, and dealing with unexpected issues during the campaign.
