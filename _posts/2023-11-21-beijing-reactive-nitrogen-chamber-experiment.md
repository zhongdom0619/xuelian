In 2023, I participated in a chamber experiment in **Beijing**, focusing on the comparison of instruments for measuring reactive nitrogen species.

Reactive nitrogen measurements are important for atmospheric chemistry, but they can be challenging because different instruments may have different sensitivities, interferences, and calibration requirements.

During this experiment, I gained experience in chamber operation, instrument comparison, and data quality control under controlled experimental conditions.
