In summer of 2021, I participated in a field measurement campaign at **Mount Tai**, a mountain site that is useful for studying atmospheric processes under elevated and regionally influenced conditions.

Mountain sites are important in atmospheric chemistry because they can capture air masses affected by regional transport, boundary-layer evolution, and photochemical processing.

During this campaign, I gained experience in field operation, instrument maintenance, and atmospheric data collection.

This campaign was also an important early step in my research training. It helped me build a foundation for later studies on HONO.
