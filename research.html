--- layout: default title: Research navbar_title: Research --- Research My research focuses on atmospheric nitrous acid (HONO), with particular interests in its sources, budgets, and impacts on atmospheric oxidation chemistry in coastal, marine, and polluted environments. Research Overview HONO is an important precursor of hydroxyl radicals (OH), which control the oxidation capacity of the lower atmosphere. However, HONO sources and budgets remain uncertain in many atmospheric environments, especially during daytime and in coastal or marine-influenced regions. My first-author and co-first-author work investigates HONO from three connected perspectives: field budgets in coastal atmosphere, HONO behavior across contrasting environments in North China, and previously unrecognized marine biological sources. 1. HONO Budgets in the Coastal Atmosphere Coastal atmosphere is influenced by both continental pollution and marine processes. In this work, we examined HONO budgets in the coastal atmosphere using field observations and chemical box modelling, with a focus on understanding daytime HONO sources and their potential marine contribution. Paper: Zhong, X. et al. Nitrous acid budgets in the coastal atmosphere: potential daytime marine sources. Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics, 2023. Read the paper 2. Atmospheric HONO in Contrasting Environments HONO chemistry can vary substantially among different atmospheric environments. This work investigates atmospheric nitrous acid in contrasting environments in North China, helping to clarify how HONO sources, budgets, and atmospheric roles change under different pollution and environmental conditions. Paper: Zhong, X. et al. Atmospheric Nitrous Acid in Contrasting Environments in North China. Environmental Science & Technology, 2026. Read the paper 3. HONO Emission from Marine Algae Marine biological processes may provide previously overlooked sources of reactive nitrogen. In this work, we investigated HONO emission from marine algae and evaluated its potential implications for coastal atmospheric oxidation chemistry. Paper: Shen, H.*, Zhong, X.* et al. HONO emission from marine algae. Science Advances, 2026. Read the paper Research Methods My research combines field measurements, chamber experiments, and chemical box modelling. I have experience with instruments and analytical methods including LOPAP, ToF-CIMS, HCHO analyzer, NO2 analyzer, and ion chromatography.
